Twilight of a preconceived notion

In the twilight of a preconceived notionI am beginning to senseWhat I should write downThat sometimes waves comeand you can braceand they will still sweep you awaywhen you are caught in pastel raysgazing on scarlet mountainsthose vineyards long overgrowncoating the village ina sweet ruby glazeIf you are there,you should first breathe,and you should know.it is magic.that is to saya science one cannot comprehendSilky air finds a thousand finsdrawn toward a setting horizonFor in the evening the illusion of separation of space and skyceases to overcome our notionswe find ourselves face to face with the stars and that great expansealas nary a barrier to pen us in at allHold onto a talisman and we may stave off busy roads, no freewaysof a city that I will tell you is but a thousand tiny townsThey’ve no space at those tables anyhowBetter to prowl with the jaguars at the barYou’ll find there is no need for sleepin a night that ought to have no endso in the morningmake a dealto plan a planand

blow.it.all.off.

take a leap,I’ll follow you strangerTo chase the setting sunBeyond the inertiaof whatwe were meant to betogether.

Does it scar?

Does it scar?Keloids round and bumpysmooth to the touch,run your fingers along,feel them under your thumb

Does it remember?Sensitized like the cold shock to an open crownLet it twitch, measure the response,potentials flow with the stimuli long gone

Does it calcify?See it on the screen, ghostly white fragments strewn about, comminutedSet in place, motion forever restrained,fortified to never fracture again in quite the same way

No no,I should have hoped it would be beyond these mortal coils,But if it should be of flesh then let it take form!programmed to shed in order to shapeIn utero a dream, webbing lost, but gaining the possibility to reach

The Copernican Principle

Violet hues and golden skies cascading,I chanced to glimpse the sunfor the first time as it truly wasacceleration and a drop through my core-the earth had finally given waySet in rotation,I fell perpetually onward..

O Copernicus, Copernicus!How did it feel when you set it all in motion?Cast out from the center,a drop on a blue dot

But in free fall, I may be finally ableto seeHeavenly bodies materialize in a realmThat may not be out of reach...You cast off the firmamentthat separates us from the stars.

And so I must conjecture an addendum,My dear Copernicus,You see I do indeed live in a privileged frameStuck in an inertial reference,I can gaze upon lightThrust at once into this new day

Thymatic

Forgive me for rushingThis river grows wildI can’t sit,I can’t settle down

At night, my restless heartkeeps me off the groundSuspended in a dreamOr maybe stranded in delusion

To see it all, a sandbox,Ozymandias building,lest it all fallsin heaps strewn around.

She said,“I hear you want to change the world,and that’s just fine.”Folly—those who would lift a finger.

Red and sable silt, stratified in the sedimentwhat could drive youup against the illsto rake the muck

O fated muse,sing of the ragethat dwells within menthematic killers,bred and selected within.

Thymosdestined to overcome.

Does this mandala’s sandnot abrade and corrode?Oh noit only fills one with grit,multiplied as the seashore,to the stars in heaven.
