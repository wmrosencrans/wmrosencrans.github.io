04 · Golgi apparatus

News

The Golgi packages cargo into vesicles and ships it out to the rest of the cell. Here’s what’s shipping.

  1. 2026

    Invited talk at the Autophagy Gordon Research Conference

    Spoke in Ventura, CA on how STING is suppressed by an ATG9A-ATG13-ATG101-dependent Golgi-to-lysosome degradation pathway that restrains chronic inflammation.

  2. January 2026

    VDAC isoforms paper in JBC

    Our co-first-author study showing that the three mammalian VDAC isoforms distinctly regulate mitochondrial function, the proteome, and cell metabolism is out in the Journal of Biological Chemistry. Read it.

  3. August 2025

    Parkinson’s drug candidates aren’t what they seem

    Our Science Advances paper found that two reported PINK1/Parkin activators boost mitophagy by mildly poisoning mitochondria rather than by activating the pathway directly. Covered by Caltech News, Medical Xpress, and the Chen Institute. Read the paper.

  4. June 2025

    Back to the NIH

    Now as a postdoc, continuing my work in Richard Youle’s lab at NINDS.

  5. June 2025

    PhD, Caltech

    Graduated with a PhD in Biochemistry and Molecular Biophysics after defending my thesis, Mechanisms of Pharmacological and Cellular Regulators of Mitophagy.

  6. April 2025

    VDAC2 paper out in Science Advances

    Our study of how VDAC2’s conformational plasticity controls its interactions with cytosolic proteins is published. Read it.

  7. 2023

    Student Bioenergeticist Award

    Recognized by the Biophysical Society for contributions to mitochondrial biophysics, with an award talk: “The mitochondrial VDAC isoforms: one molecule at a time.”

  8. December 2020

    Featured by Colgate

    Colgate profiled how my undergraduate research, from Singapore to the NIH, led into my PhD work on mitophagy. Read the story.
